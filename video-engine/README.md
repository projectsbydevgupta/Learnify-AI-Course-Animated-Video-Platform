# 🎬 Learnify Video Engine — Remotion & React 19

The programmatic video rendering engine for **Learnify**, built with **Remotion (v4.0)** and **React 19**.

---

## 🎨 Overview

The video engine converts structured scene blueprints (`temp_props_{topic_id}.json`) generated by the `VisualPlannerAgent` into broadcast-quality 1080p animated MP4 videos with synchronized AI voiceovers.

## ⏱️ Audio-Scene Synchronization Mechanism

1. **Centisecond Measurement**: `mutagen` measures spoken audio files down to the centisecond.
2. **Buffer Allocation**: A `+0.4s` breathing room buffer is added to each scene.
3. **Lockstep Sequence**: In `src/Root.tsx`, each scene graphic and its corresponding audio voiceover are mounted inside the exact same `<Sequence from={startFrame} durationInFrames={durationInFrames}>` at 30 FPS.
4. **Zero Drift**: Scene transitions and audio voiceovers reset dynamically per scene, eliminating cumulative desynchronization.
